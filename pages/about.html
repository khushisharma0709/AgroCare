About AgroCare

AgroCare is a student-friendly smart farming project that brings common agriculture tools into one simple website.

Mission

Help farmers make faster decisions about crop health, weather risk, seasonal planning, and available support.

Future scope

Add real AI disease detection, live weather APIs, user accounts, multilingual content, and official scheme integrations.
